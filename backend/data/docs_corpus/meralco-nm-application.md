Apply for Net Metering

Prepare the current forms and supporting documents, complete Meralco’s technical review and inspection, and commission the approved system safely.

Download the application form

On this page

Your application journey

Application

Complete the current form and submit the initial account, applicant, project, equipment, provider, and authorization documents through an approved channel.

Evaluation

Meralco completes the Distribution Impact Study and determines whether further measurement, a Distribution Asset Study, or network work is required.

Inspection

Coordinate the service-entrance inspection, complete required corrections, and secure the applicable LGU permits and Certificate of Final Electrical Inspection.

Completion

Submit the remaining documents, sign the Net Metering Agreement and Fixed-Asset Boundary Document, and settle applicable fees.

Energization and commissioning

Meralco installs or configures the approved meters and completes grid-safety testing and commissioning before parallel operation.

Transmittal

Meralco transmits the completed Net Metering documents and payment record to the Energy Regulatory Commission for Certificate of Compliance processing.
