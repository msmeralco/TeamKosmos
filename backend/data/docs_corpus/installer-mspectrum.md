What MSpectrum's website says about itself: MSpectrum is a solar power company built with Meralco's energy expertise, and its news items describe it as the wholly owned solar subsidiary of the Manila Electric Company (Meralco). It offers solar for homes, solar for business, and microgrid and utility-scale projects. Its residential page lists net metering, zero export and peak/off-peak options. It says that since 2020 it has installed solar for around 38,000 houses, schools and community centers (a self-reported figure).

It also publishes articles on choosing a solar company and on why the lowest quote does not always mean lower energy costs.

Sol note: MSpectrum, Inc. also appears on both versions of the DOE Solar PV Installer Registry (Metro Manila). This entry only records who the company says it is. Sol has not checked its prices, quality or customer reviews, and does not have its rates. Ask it for a written quote like any other installer, and compare.
