Sol note: firms listed in the Department of Energy's Official Solar PV Installer Registry for Metro Manila (NCR), Central Luzon (Region III) and CALABARZON (Region IV-A), the regions that overlap Meralco's service area. The DOE published two versions: one updated as of Jan 31, 2026 (92 installers and 14 energy service companies) and one updated as of Jul 13, 2026 (36 installers and 15 energy service companies). The newer list is shorter, and the two do not match, so a firm missing from one may still be registered.

The DOE says the registry is a reference for government entities that buy solar for their own buildings. It states that the DOE does not endorse any listed firm. Being listed means registered, not checked for price, quality or service. Sol has not vetted any of these firms and does not rank them. Ask any firm for its current DOE registration and a written quote.

Sources: the Jan 31, 2026 PDF at https://prod-cms.doe.gov.ph/documents/d/eumb/list-of-solar-pv-installer-registry-as-of-31-january-2026-pdf and the Jul 13, 2026 PDF, which carries the same title as the DOE page https://doe.gov.ph/official-doe-solar-pv-installer-registry-updated-as-of-july-13-2026.
