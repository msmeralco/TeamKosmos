Supporting the Push for Solar and Renewables

More Filipino homes and businesses are choosing solar to lower their electricity bills while helping protect the environment. With abundant sunlight almost year-round, the Philippines has exceptional potential to turn sunshine into clean energy and savings.

Start with the connection your system needs

Net Metering is intended for eligible renewable-energy installations within current capacity limits that can export unused generation through an approved grid connection.

If you plan to export excess energy

Use the Solar and Net Metering guide to understand billing, metering, safety, technical review, and the application process.

If the proposed system is larger or will not export

Review the Distributed Energy Resources, SGF Zero Export or Micro Solar System guidance for the requirements that apply to your system.

If you are still comparing systems

Review daytime electricity use, site conditions, system design, provider capability, and after-sales support before committing to an installation.
