Sol's design catalog (the panels, inverters and batteries behind the calculated options and their prices) is synthetic demo data, labeled DemoSolar, DemoVolt and DemoCell. Those prices are placeholders for the calculations, not real quotes. The separate "ready-made packages" tables are different: they list packages that real installers publish on their own websites, with their published prices, a link and a photo where the installer has one. Those prices are not quotes either. No retailer source is enabled for live prices, so Sol cannot state a current market price for any single real product. It can only show the package prices installers publish.

The product pages in this library (for example JinkoSolar and Huawei) are manufacturer marketing pages. They describe features and model ranges. They do not give Philippine prices, and a mention here is not a recommendation. Specifications on those pages are the manufacturer's own claims. For a real purchase, compare the datasheet, the warranty terms and the installer's written quote.

Sunlight data comes from PVGIS-ERA5, a modelled dataset, and every result is an estimate for a licensed installer to confirm.
